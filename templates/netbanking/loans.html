How a loan works here

  • A bank officer checks your application. If it is approved, the money is paid into your account.
  • You pay the same EMI every month. Each EMI pays the interest for that month, the rest reduces the loan.
  • The first EMI is due one month after the payout.
  • Each EMI is taken from your account automatically on its due date (auto-debit). You can also pay it earlier yourself.
  • If there is not enough money on the due date, a late fee of {{ late_fee }}% of the EMI is added and we try again every day.
  • You can close the loan early: you pay what is left plus interest up to that day, no extra charge.
  • Amount: {{ LOAN_MIN|money }} to {{ LOAN_MAX|money }}. At most 2 loans at a time (active or waiting).

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n - 1), r = yearly rate / 12, n = months.
